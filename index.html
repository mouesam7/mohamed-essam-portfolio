Case study — 01

More than a coaching website.

Coach Mohamed Ahmed started as a website brief. After mapping the actual business, it became a complete operational product: one place for orders, payment review, confidential client intake, programs and subscriptions.

01

The challenge

Coaching operations were spread across messages, payment proofs, forms and manual follow-ups. That made it harder to see where each client was in the process.

02

The system

A bilingual, mobile-first platform with pricing by country, a guided payment flow, private assessment forms, automated updates and an admin dashboard for the coach.

03

What it enables

A clear path from visitor to active client — with less manual tracking, a more professional brand experience and room to scale.

The client journey

Designed around what actually happens.

  1. DiscoverSee the coach, results, plans and the right price for their country.
  2. SubscribeChoose a plan, apply a discount if available and upload payment proof.
  3. ReviewThe coach reviews payment while both sides receive clear email updates.
  4. AssessThe client completes a private coaching form and submits body photos securely.
  5. DeliverAfter WhatsApp delivery, the coach starts the subscription and tracking begins automatically.

What makes it dependable

The details that protect the real workflow.

The product was designed for the situations that happen outside the ideal path: repeated submits, unstable connections, large mobile uploads and sensitive information. Orders use idempotency protection and database transactions; private uploads use secure storage and temporary links; the admin side uses role-based access, MFA and validation.
