## Featured case study

### Mohamed Ahmed — Online Coaching Platform

An end-to-end bilingual coaching platform and private operations system. It was designed around the real journey from first visit through payment review, private client intake, program delivery and subscription tracking.

Key product decisions:

- Country-based pricing and payment options for Egypt and international visitors
- Payment-proof review and automated lifecycle email updates
- Private intake forms and media uploads for sensitive client information
- Coach-facing dashboard for orders, clients, programs, plans, subscriptions and feedback
- Idempotent checkout, server-side validation, private storage and role-based access
- Arabic RTL and English LTR experience, built mobile-first

**Live project:** [mahmedahmed.com/ar](https://www.mahmedahmed.com/ar)
